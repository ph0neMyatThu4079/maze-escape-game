📖 How to Play Maze Escape

🎯 Objective

Navigate through the randomly generated maze from start (top-left) to exit (bottom-right) before time runs out!

💰 How to Play

  1. Place Your Bet: Click chips ($1, $5, $25, $50, $100, $500) to add them to your bet
  2. Start Maze: Click "Start Maze" button
  3. Random Difficulty: Game randomly selects Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty
  4. Navigate: Use arrow keys (⬆️⬇️⬅️➡️) or WASD to move through the maze
  5. Reach Exit: Get to the gold door (🚪) before time runs out!
  6. Win/Lose: Win multiplied bet if you escape, lose bet if time runs out

🎲 Difficulty Levels

Easy 🟢

  • Maze Size: 15x15
  • Time Limit: 40 seconds
  • Win Multiplier: 1.5x
  • Example: Bet $100 → Win $150

Medium 🟡

  • Maze Size: 20x20
  • Time Limit: 30 seconds
  • Win Multiplier: 2x
  • Example: Bet $100 → Win $200

Hard 🔴

  • Maze Size: 25x25
  • Time Limit: 20 seconds
  • Win Multiplier: 3x
  • Example: Bet $100 → Win $300

🕹️ Controls

Keyboard Controls:

  • Arrow Keys: ⬆️ Up, ⬇️ Down, ⬅️ Left, ➡️ Right
  • WASD Keys: W (Up), A (Left), S (Down), D (Right)

On-Screen Controls:

  • Click the arrow buttons below the maze
  • Works on mobile and tablets

🏆 Winning & Losing

You Win! 🎉

  • Reach the exit (🚪) before time runs out
  • Get your bet back + winnings (1.5x, 2x, or 3x)
  • Bank balance increases

You Lose 😢

  • Time runs out before reaching exit
  • Click "Give Up" button
  • Lose your bet amount

⚠️ Important Notes

  • Random Difficulty: You don't choose the difficulty - it's random each game!
  • Unique Mazes: Every maze is randomly generated - never the same twice
  • Timer Warning: Bar turns yellow at 10s, red (flashing) at 5s
  • Cannot Move Through Walls: Black walls block your path
  • Bankruptcy Protection: If you lose all money, bank resets to $1000

💡 Pro Tips

  • Look Ahead: Plan your route before moving
  • Stay Calm: Don't panic when timer gets low
  • Bet Smart: Harder mazes pay more, but are riskier
  • Practice: Learn maze patterns to solve faster
